My decision to pursue General Engineering at DTU comes from a deeply personal journey shaped by resilience and growth. After not being admitted in 2025, I strengthened my skills in scientific writing, embedded systems, and C++ programming and also attending seminars through my Danish labor union, such as:

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{AI-Driven Protein Engineering} by Professor Timothy P. Jenkins from DTU, exploring how protein design can be transformed into tools shaping the future of protein engineering.
\item \textbf{Designing Proteins for the Future} by Professor Dek Woolfson at the University of Copenhagen, focusing on creating novel proteins with new functions and applications.
\item \textbf{CRE - Facts and Prevention} by Associate Professor Barbara Holzknecht at the University of Copenhagen, highlighting the emerging threat of carbapenem-resistant enterobacteriaceae and strategies to combat it.

I have carefully reviewed the curriculum and developed a study plan. I want to specialize in living systems and will take the relevant coursework. In the 1st semester, I will start by taking \enquote{12701 Introduction to Living systems} and \enquote{02135 Introduction to Cyber Systems} on the 2nd semester. I also plan to take \enquote{22111 Bioinformatics} in the 3rd semester, along with \enquote{27020 Bioengineering}. In my 4th semester, I plan to take \enquote{28025 Bio Process Technology}, \enquote{26211 Biophysics and Biophysical Chemistry}, and \enquote{27034 Fermentation Technology}. I believe these courses are highly relevant to my future plans. For the 5th semester, I plan to apply for an exchange program to gain an international perspective and research experience abroad. I also look forward to the 3-week project format and am excited about participating in \enquote{27016 Design-Build 1: Devices for Measuring Cell Growth} and \enquote{10240 Design-Build 2: Rational Catalyst Design}.

My goal is to pursue a Master's in Biomedical Engineering and contribute as a researcher to developing innovative biomedical technologies, including AI-assisted diagnostics and human augmentation solutions. I aim to work in Novo Nordisk, Eurofins and Coloplast. I am particularly motivated to address Denmark's societal challenges, such as supporting an aging population and promoting sustainable, eco-friendly medical technologies.
