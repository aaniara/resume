\opening{Dear Admissions Committee,}

My decision to pursue General Engineering at Technical University of Denmark comes from a deeply personal journey shaped by curiosity, setbacks, and growth. I have always been drawn to science, which led me to complete a Bachelor’s degree in Pharmacy. However, over time, I realized that while pharmacy gave me a strong foundation, I wanted to go beyond understanding systems. I wanted to design, innovate, and build solutions. Engineering represents that opportunity.

When I was not admitted in 2025, it was disappointing, but it also became a turning point. Instead of seeing it as a failure, I chose to see it as feedback. After speaking with the Head of Studies, I understood where I needed to improve, and I committed myself to doing so. Over the past year, I have worked while actively continuing to learn, attending seminars through my Danish labour union, including those focused on protein design in collaboration with researchers from Technical University of Denmark and University of Copenhagen. These experiences made me realize how powerful the combination of health sciences and engineering can be, and they sparked my interest in living systems.

At the same time, I began to feel a growing frustration. In my daily work, I often found myself curious about how technologies function, how systems are designed, and how solutions are created, but I could only go so far with my current knowledge. That feeling of limitation became a strong motivation. I did not just want to observe or assist, but I wanted to understand deeply and contribute actively.

To take steps in that direction, I started teaching myself skills such as scientific writing, embedded systems, and C++ programming, with guidance from my partner, a DTU graduate. These small but meaningful steps made me realize how much I enjoy problem-solving and working with technology. They also confirmed that engineering is not just an interest, but it is something I am ready to fully commit to.

My academic motivation is also closely connected to my future goals. I am particularly interested in specializing in Living Systems or Future Energy. Living systems appeal to me because of my background in health and biology, and my interest in areas like protein design and biotechnology. At the same time, future energy represents a field where engineering can directly address global challenges and create sustainable solutions. Both areas inspire me because they combine innovation with real impact. I attended a seminar at Copenhagen Atomics on thorium-based nuclear energy, which contributed to my understanding of energy technologies. In addition, I have used learning materials provided through my network, and participated in courses organized by Pharmadanmark.

This past year has also been one of the most challenging periods of my life. Alongside academic rejection, I faced a serious health challenge battling cancer and am now cured. Going through this experience changed my perspective. It made me more aware of how I want to spend my time and what kind of future I want to build. It strengthened my resilience and gave me a deeper sense of purpose. I no longer see obstacles as something that stops me, but as something that shapes me.

Today, I feel more prepared and more certain than ever. I am not applying simply because I am interested in engineering. I am applying because I have tested my motivation, worked on my weaknesses, and grown through difficult experiences. I am ready to take on the challenges of the General Engineering program at DTU and to continue developing both academically and personally.
\closing{Sincerely, \\
Applicant}
